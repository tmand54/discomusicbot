## Full description (max 4000)

```
Scan a stack of cards in seconds and see what your collection is worth.

TCG Investment Portfolio reads the card number right on your phone, so cards are recognized as fast as you can slide them under the camera, even with a weak signal. Download a set once and scanning works fully offline.

FAST SCANNING
• Rapid mode: keep sliding cards under the camera and each one is added as it's recognized
• Single mode: check each card before it's added
• Binder mode: one photo of a binder page adds every pocket (2×2, 3×3, 3×4 or 4×4)
• Picks the right printing by comparing the artwork: parallels, alt arts, Judge and promo versions
• Snap a full-resolution photo for tricky cards, or type the number

PRICES AND PORTFOLIO
• Market prices for every printing, updated daily
• Total value, ROI, and profit and loss against what you paid
• Performance chart for 1 day, 5 days, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year or all time, with % change
• Track pack openings: enter what you paid and see whether the rip came out ahead
• Price alerts when a card rises above or falls below your target

COLLECTION
• Set progress with the cards you're missing, plus a wantlist
• Condition, grading, purchase price, language and notes for every copy
• Search by any words: name, set, number, rarity or printing (for example "robin eb03 sp")
• Download sets for offline use
• Import a pasted card list, a CSV, or a backup from another collection app
• Back up to a file and export to CSV whenever you like

GAMES
Pokémon TCG, Magic: The Gathering, Disney Lorcana, One Piece Card Game (English and Japanese, including DON!! cards), Riftbound and Dragon Ball Super Card Game (Fusion World and Masters).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no ads. Your collection stays on your phone. Card text is read on your phone; only the card number or name is sent to look the card up.

TCG Investment Portfolio is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Wizards of the Coast, Disney, Ravensburger, Bandai, Riot Games or TCGplayer. Card names and images belong to their respective owners.
```

### Screenshots to take

Take them on your phone (Power + Volume down). Use your own cards on a plain table and avoid
anything with personal details. Recommended order:

1. **Rapid scan in action**: camera with a card in the frame and the live "found" banner.
2. **Scan session**: the review sheet with several cards and the total value.
3. **Portfolio**: the total value, the chart and P&L.
4. **Card details**: prices for each printing and the price history.
5. **Collection / set progress**: a set with owned and missing cards.
6. **Binder mode**: a binder page with every pocket recognized.

Tip: Play rejects screenshots that are mostly third-party logos or that imply endorsement. Keep the
app's UI the focus, as the big scanner apps do.
