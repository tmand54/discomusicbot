# TCG Investment Portfolio — Privacy Policy

_Effective November 22, 2026_

TCG Investment Portfolio is a trading card scanner and collection tracker. It has no accounts, no ads and no
analytics of its own. Your collection stays on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

* **Your collection**: cards, quantities, conditions, purchase prices, grading details, notes,
  wantlist, price alerts and value history. It is stored only in the app's private storage.
* **Camera images**: the scanner reads card text on your phone with Google's on-device ML Kit.
  Camera frames and photos are never saved or uploaded. A small, low-resolution copy of the
  card's artwork is kept in memory only while a scan session is open, to choose between printings.
* **Your settings.**

The app doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or other personal information.

## What leaves your phone, and why

To identify cards and show prices, the app requests card data from these services:

| Service | What is sent |
| --- | --- |
| TCGdex (Pokémon), Scryfall (Magic), YGOPRODeck (Yu-Gi-Oh!), Lorcast (Lorcana) | The card number, set code or name being looked up, and search text you type |
| tcgcsv.com (TCGplayer catalog) and optcgapi.com | Requests for set lists and card prices, and One Piece search text |
| Card image hosts of the services above, and onepiece-cardgame.com | Requests for card images |

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address and the app's name and
version. They don't receive your collection, and the app sends no identifier that links your
requests together. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

**Google ML Kit.** The on-device text reader is Google's ML Kit. According to Google, ML Kit sends
device information (such as manufacturer, model and OS version), app information (package name and
version) and performance metrics to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. This data is
encrypted in transit and isn't shared with third parties. Card images and the text read from them are
processed on your phone and aren't sent to Google.

**Links you open.** Links to TCGplayer, eBay or Yuyu-tei open in your browser, under that site's own privacy policy.

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on, Android can include the app's data in your device backup, which is
stored in your own Google account. Backup files you create with **Back up collection** or **Export
CSV** are saved wherever you choose, and the app doesn't upload them.

## Permissions

* **Camera**: to scan cards. Scanning is the only use of the camera.
* **Notifications**: optional, for the price alerts you set.
* **Internet**: to look up cards and prices.

## Children

TCG Investment Portfolio isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information
from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored.
**Settings → Delete collection** deletes your collection, rip history and value history (and, if
you choose, your wantlist and price alerts). **Settings → Clear offline card data** removes downloaded
card data and keeps your collection.
